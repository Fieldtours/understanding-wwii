## Not fully verified (editor note)
- Spitfire Mk I ammunition (300 rounds a gun, about 15 seconds' firing) and the Panzerfaust 60 warhead diameter shown as "wider than the tube" (no figure given) are standard figures from memory, not rechecked online.
- The date of the Bf 109E's forced landing in Sussex is not given in the card; Wikipedia confirms only that it flew in the Battle of Britain and belly-landed in Sussex.
- The Into Europe field names were kept (`desc`, `versions`, `story`, `lugares`) so the existing renderer works unchanged; the brief's "changes / anecdote / places" map to `versions` / `story` / `lugares`.
