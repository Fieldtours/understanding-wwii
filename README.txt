UNDERSTANDING WWII - THE SECOND WORLD WAR IN EUROPE 1939-1945 - A DZ CHARLIE FIELD GUIDE

WHAT IT IS
A free phone guide to the whole war in Europe, from the first shots at Westerplatte
(1 September 1939) to the German surrender (8 May 1945): 160 sites in 15 areas, from
Britain to Russia and from Norway to Malta. 63 tour days, 11 ready-made tours, 54 museums,
63 cemeteries, memorials and camps, a 65-event timeline, 12 step-by-step battle maps,
weapons and equipment, 36 key figures and the armies.
In English. Built 19 Oct 2026 on the Into Europe / Easy Company template.
Short name (slug): understanding-wwii

VERSION
v1 - 19 October 2026 - first version
     - Tour page: Ready-made (11 tours: the whole war in 52 days, Britain, Dunkirk & Normandy,
       France, the Bulge/Rhine/Arnhem, Germany, Poland, Prague to the Alps, Italy/Malta/Crete,
       Norway, the Eastern Front) / By area (15 areas) / Build my own (up to 21 days)
     - Trip diary: "Despatches" (the reports sent home from the front), stamp SEEN.
       Quiet pages at camps and cemeteries (no quiz question there).
     - Look: the war-room briefing - slate blue, plotting-table red, linen paper,
       Big Shoulders Stencil lettering. Cover photos: IWM CH 11887 and CH 7698 (public domain).
     - Google-style road maps of all Europe: overview + 17 regions + 66 middle areas +
       89 close-ups (622 pictures, 46 MB). "Save for offline" saves everything; a second
       button saves only the maps for your tour (smaller).
     - 9 GPS pins corrected after an independent check (Research/gps-check.csv, gps-moves.csv)
     - "Where the sources disagree" on 90 stops (list: Research/accuracy-check.md)
     - "Report a problem" on every stop (to jj28@posteo.uk)
     - PDF in the house layout: 177 pages, 5.8 MB
     - Visitor counter: counted as "Understanding WWII" on your numbers page
     - Tested: every ready-made tour, every area, build-my-own, the diary, search, offline,
       dark mode, phone and computer screens - no errors.
     Map data: OpenStreetMap contributors (via OpenFreeMap).
